LARKSPUR SCHEDULER - SUPPORT FAQ (for customers)

When is support available?
Support is available Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 UTC. Customers on the Premium plan also have an emergency email address for outages outside those hours.

How can I contact support?
By email (help@larkspur.example) or in the chat inside the dashboard. We do not offer phone support.

What does each plan cost?
Basic: 79 USD per clinic per month. Plus: 149 USD per clinic per month. Premium: 299 USD per clinic per month. New customers get a 30 day free trial.

Can I get a refund?
Yes. Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refunded for the current month.

How do I reset my password?
Use "Forgot password" on the login page. The reset link is valid for 60 minutes. If your clinic uses single sign-on, ask your administrator.

Can patients pay through the booking widget?
No. Scheduler does not process payments.

Where is my data stored?
In the European Union only.
