LARKSPUR SYSTEMS - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Edition 2026 (applies to all full-time staff)

1. About this handbook
Larkspur Systems builds appointment scheduling software for small medical clinics. This handbook explains how we work together. It does not replace your employment contract. If the two disagree, your contract applies.

2. Working hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you may arrange your day freely, as long as your team lead knows when you are reachable. Nobody is expected to answer messages after 19:00 unless they are on call.

3. Where we work
Larkspur is hybrid. Every employee spends at least two days per week in the Lisbon office, usually Tuesday and Thursday. Employees who live more than 150 km from Lisbon are remote and visit the office for the quarterly team week instead.

4. Leave
Annual leave is 22 working days per calendar year. Leave does not carry over, except up to 5 days that may be moved to the first quarter of the next year with written approval from the team lead.
Sick leave is 10 paid days per year. A medical certificate is needed from the third consecutive day of absence.
Parental leave follows national law; Larkspur adds two extra paid weeks for each parent.

5. Probation and notice
The probation period is three months. During probation either side may end the contract with 7 days notice. After probation the notice period is 30 days for the employee and 60 days for the company.

6. Equipment
Every employee receives a laptop and a monitor. Laptops must be returned within 14 days after the last working day. Personal devices may not store customer data.

7. Conduct
We do not tolerate harassment or discrimination. Concerns can be raised with your team lead, with People Operations, or anonymously through the form on the intranet. Reports are acknowledged within two working days.

8. Questions
Write to people-ops@larkspur.example. The handbook is reviewed every January.
