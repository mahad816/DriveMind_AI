LARKSPUR SYSTEMS - OFFICE HOLIDAYS 2026 (Lisbon)

The Lisbon office is closed on:
- 1 January (New Year)
- 3 April (Good Friday)
- 25 April (Freedom Day)
- 1 May (Labour Day)
- 10 June (Portugal Day)
- 15 August (Assumption)
- 25 December (Christmas)

In addition, the company is closed on 24 December and 31 December.
Support continues on closed days with reduced hours; the on-call engineer remains reachable.
Annual leave requests for the weeks of 20 July and 3 August must be submitted by 15 June.
