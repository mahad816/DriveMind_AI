CUSTOMER FEEDBACK SUMMARY - FIRST HALF OF 2026
Prepared by Mei Tanaka (Sales Manager). Ratings use a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).

Five clinics were interviewed.
- Alder Clinic: 4.5. Likes the waiting list. Wants a mobile app.
- Birch Dental: 3.0. Reminders by SMS arrive late at times. Considering a competitor.
- Cedar Pediatrics: 5.0. Praises support speed and the booking widget.
- Dune Physio: 2.5. Unhappy with the calendar sync before release 1.3.
- Elm Family Practice: 4.0. Wants payment at booking time.

Average rating of the five interviews: 3.8.
Net Promoter Score across the whole customer base: 31.

Themes
1. Mobile app is the most requested feature (three of five clinics).
2. SMS delivery reliability is the main complaint.
3. Payments at booking time were requested by one clinic; this is not on the roadmap.

Follow-up
