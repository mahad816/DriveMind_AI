CLOUDHOST GMBH - HOSTING AGREEMENT, SUMMARY FOR INTERNAL USE
Contract owner at Larkspur: Joao Ferreira (Finance Lead)

Parties: Larkspur Systems Lda. and CloudHost GmbH
Service: managed hosting of the production environment in the Frankfurt region (EU).

Term
The agreement runs for 24 months, from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2027.

Fees
Monthly fee: 4,200 USD, invoiced at the start of each month. Annual price increases are capped at 3 percent.

Service levels
CloudHost guarantees 99.95 percent monthly availability of the hosting platform.
If availability falls below 99.95 percent, Larkspur receives a service credit of 10 percent of the monthly fee. Below 99.0 percent the credit is 25 percent.

Termination
Either party may terminate with 90 days written notice, effective no earlier than 31 December 2026. Larkspur may terminate immediately if CloudHost suffers a security breach that exposes customer data.

Data
CloudHost acts as a processor. The data processing agreement is attached as Annex B. Data stays in the Frankfurt region.

Support
Premium support is included with a two-hour response time for severity 1 tickets.
