LARKSPUR SYSTEMS - EXPENSE POLICY
Version 1.0, effective 1 January 2024
Status: superseded by Version 2.0 on 1 March 2026 (see expense_policy_v2.txt)

Purpose
This policy tells employees which business costs are reimbursed and how to claim them.

Meals
Meals while travelling on company business are reimbursed up to 25 USD per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

Approvals
Any single expense above 200 USD needs written approval from your manager before you spend the money. Expenses above 1,000 USD also need approval from the Finance Lead.

Submitting claims
Claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of the expense date. Late claims are paid only with the Finance Lead's written exception.

Receipts
A receipt is required for every expense above 10 USD. For smaller amounts a short note is enough.

Mileage
Business use of a private car is reimbursed at 0.30 USD per kilometre.

Reimbursement timing
Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll run.
