LARKSPUR SYSTEMS - TRAVEL POLICY
Reviewed March 2026. Read together with the expense policy.

Booking
All travel is booked through the company travel desk (travel@larkspur.example) at least 14 days before departure. Trips booked later need the approval of the department head.

Flights
Economy class is the default. Business class is allowed only when a single flight is longer than 6 hours and the department head has approved it in writing.

Hotels
The nightly hotel limit is 150 USD. In San Francisco and New York the limit is 220 USD per night. Hotels must be booked through the travel desk so that invoices are issued to Larkspur Systems.

Trains and local transport
Second class rail is the default. Taxis are allowed when public transport is unsafe, unavailable, or when luggage makes it impractical. Ride-hailing receipts must show the route.

Per diem and meals
We do not pay a flat per diem. Meals are reimbursed as described in the current expense policy.

Customer visits
For customer visits of more than three days, the account owner must send an itinerary to the Operations Manager before departure.

Insurance
Larkspur holds a group travel insurance policy. Claims must be reported to the travel desk within 48 hours of the incident.
