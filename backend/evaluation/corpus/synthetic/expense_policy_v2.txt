LARKSPUR SYSTEMS - EXPENSE POLICY
Version 2.0, effective 1 March 2026
This version replaces Version 1.0 (effective 1 January 2024). Claims for expenses incurred before 1 March 2026 are still judged under Version 1.0.

Purpose
This policy tells employees which business costs are reimbursed and how to claim them.

Meals
Meals while travelling on company business are reimbursed up to 40 USD per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Team dinners organised by a team lead are reimbursed up to 55 USD per person.

Approvals
Any single expense above 300 USD needs written approval from your manager before you spend the money. Expenses above 1,500 USD also need approval from the Finance Lead.

Submitting claims
Claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 45 days of the expense date. Late claims are paid only with the Finance Lead's written exception.

Receipts
A receipt is required for every expense above 15 USD. For smaller amounts a short note is enough.

Mileage
Business use of a private car is reimbursed at 0.35 USD per kilometre.

Reimbursement timing
Approved claims are paid within 10 working days. Payment no longer waits for the monthly payroll run.

Home office
A one-time home office allowance of 250 USD is available to every employee after probation. It requires a receipt.
