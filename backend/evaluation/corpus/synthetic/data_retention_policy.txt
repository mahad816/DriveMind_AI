LARKSPUR SYSTEMS - DATA RETENTION POLICY
Approved by the Security Lead and the Finance Lead, January 2026

Application logs are kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically.
Database backups are kept for 35 days.
Customer appointment data is kept for 7 years after the customer contract ends, because clinics must meet record keeping rules. After 7 years it is deleted within 30 days.
Support tickets are kept for 3 years.
Financial records are kept for 10 years, as required by Portuguese law.
Recruitment data of candidates who were not hired is deleted after 6 months.

Deletion requests from individuals are answered within 30 days. Requests are logged by the Security Lead.

Larkspur does not keep video recordings of meetings.
