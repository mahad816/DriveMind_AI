LARKSPUR SYSTEMS - ONBOARDING CHECKLIST FOR NEW EMPLOYEES

Before day one
- People Operations sends the contract, the handbook and the equipment form.
- IT prepares the laptop and the accounts (email, repository, password manager).

Day one
- Meet your buddy. Every new employee gets a buddy who is not their direct manager.
- Collect the laptop and set up multi-factor authentication with an authenticator app.
- Read the security policy and the expense policy.

First week
- Complete the 90-minute security awareness training.
- Shadow an on-call shift (engineers) or a support shift (support staff).
- Meet the CEO for a 30 minute welcome conversation.

First 30 days
- Agree three goals with your team lead.
- Visit the Lisbon office on at least four days.
- Attend the monthly all-hands meeting, held on the first Wednesday of the month at 15:00.

Day 90
- Probation review with your team lead and People Operations.
