LARKSPUR SCHEDULER - RELEASE NOTES, VERSION 1.2
Release date: 10 February 2026

New
- Waiting list for cancelled appointments.
- Calendar sync with Google Calendar.
- Booking widget available in four languages: English, Portuguese, Spanish and French.

Changed
- The public API limit is 60 requests per minute per customer key.
- Reminder emails now show the clinic logo.

Fixed
- Time zone error for clinics that observe daylight saving time at different dates than the server.
- Duplicate reminders when an appointment was moved twice on the same day.

Known issues
- Microsoft 365 calendar sync is not available yet.
- Importing more than 20,000 rows at once can time out.
