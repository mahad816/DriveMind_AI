MARROWDALE FREIGHT COOPERATIVE - BYLAWS
Adopted at the annual general meeting of 2024.

Article 1. Name and purpose
1.1 The cooperative is called the Marrowdale Freight Cooperative and is owned by its members.
1.2 Its purpose is to move members' goods reliably and at fair cost, and to share the surplus among members.
1.3 The registered office is the Kessel Yard depot.
1.4 The financial year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

Article 2. Membership
2.1 Any haulier, producer or retailer that ships at least 40 tonnes a year may apply to join.
2.2 Applications are decided by the board within 30 days of receipt.
2.3 A member holds one share and one vote, whatever the volume it ships.
2.4 A member who ships nothing for 24 months may be removed by a board resolution.

Article 3. Fees and shares
3.1 The standard annual membership fee is 1,250 GBP, payable by 31 January. Other member classes pay the fee in the schedule of fees.
3.2 A new member buys one share at a price of 500 GBP, repaid without interest on leaving.
3.3 The board may allow payment of the fee in two instalments for members under 100 tonnes.
3.4 Unpaid fees after 90 days suspend the member's right to vote.

Article 4. Delegates
4.1 Each depot elects delegates to the general meeting, one for every 10 members based there.
4.2 Delegates serve for two years and may be re-elected twice.
4.3 A delegate who misses three meetings in a row is replaced by the depot council.
4.4 Delegates are not paid but their travel costs are reimbursed.

Article 5. General meeting
5.1 The annual general meeting is held within 5 months of the end of the financial year.
5.2 Members must receive 21 days' written notice of any general meeting.
5.3 An extraordinary meeting is called if one tenth of the members ask for it in writing.
5.4 The chair of the board chairs the meeting unless the meeting decides otherwise.

Article 6. Quorum
6.1 A general meeting needs delegates representing at least 40 percent of the members.
6.2 If the quorum is not reached, the meeting is repeated 14 days later with no quorum.
6.3 The board needs five of its seven members to be present for a valid meeting.
6.4 A depot council needs three members present.

Article 7. Voting
7.1 Ordinary resolutions pass with a simple majority of votes cast.
7.2 Changes to these bylaws need a majority of two thirds of the votes cast.
7.3 Article 7 may only be changed at an annual general meeting.
7.4 Votes are secret when 10 delegates ask for it.

Article 8. Board
8.1 The board has seven members elected by the general meeting for three-year terms.
8.2 One third of the board retires each year.
8.3 The board appoints the managing director and may dismiss them by a majority of five votes.
8.4 Board members may not hold an office in a competing carrier.

Article 9. Treasurer and audit
9.1 The treasurer is a board member and presents the accounts to the annual general meeting.
9.2 The accounts are audited every year by an auditor who is not a member.
9.3 Spending above 75,000 GBP outside the budget needs a board resolution.
9.4 The auditor's report is sent to all members with the notice of the annual general meeting.

Article 10. Surplus
10.1 At least 20 percent of the yearly surplus goes to the reserve until it equals half a year's costs.
10.2 The rest is paid back to members in proportion to the freight they shipped.
10.3 No payment is made in a year with a deficit.
10.4 A member who has left within the year receives their share of the surplus for the months served.

Article 11. Disputes
11.1 A member with a complaint writes to the managing director, who answers within 14 days.
11.2 An unresolved complaint goes to a panel of three delegates from other depots.
11.3 The panel decides within 60 days and its decision binds the cooperative.
11.4 A member may appeal the panel's decision to the general meeting.

Article 12. Conflicts of interest
12.1 A board member or delegate declares any personal interest in a matter before it is discussed.
12.2 A member with an interest does not vote on that matter and leaves the room during the vote.
12.3 A contract between the cooperative and a board member is valid only if the board approves it without that member's vote.
12.4 The secretary keeps a register of declared interests and shows it to any member on request.

Article 13. Records and information
13.1 Minutes of every board and general meeting are sent to all members within 21 days.
13.2 The membership register is kept by the secretary and is open to members during office hours.
13.3 Financial records are kept for 10 years and meeting minutes for as long as the cooperative exists.
13.4 A member may ask for a copy of the accounts at any time and receives it within 14 days.

Article 14. Dissolution
14.1 The cooperative is dissolved only by a resolution passed by three quarters of the votes cast.
14.2 After debts are paid, the remaining assets are shared among members in proportion to their shipped freight over the last five years.
14.3 The board appoints a liquidator.
14.4 These bylaws are kept in the registered office and sent to every new member.

Article 15. Depot councils
15.1 At Kessel Yard the council of 9 seats meets every first Tuesday under the chair, Odalys Venn. 5 members make a quorum, and 7 days after each meeting the minutes are sent to the board.
15.1a The Kessel Yard council elects 2 delegate(s) to the general meeting for every block of 10 members, and reports to the board by 7 January each year.
15.2 The Harbourside depot council has 5 seats, meets on the third Tuesday of each month and is chaired by Marit Lindqvist. A meeting needs 4 members present, and the minutes go to the board within 14 days.
15.2a The Harbourside council elects 2 delegate(s) to the general meeting for every block of 10 members, and reports to the board by 17 January each year.
15.3 At Northgate the council of 5 seats meets every first Tuesday under the chair, Anselm Draycott. 3 members make a quorum, and 14 days after each meeting the minutes are sent to the board.
15.3a The Northgate council elects 2 delegate(s) to the general meeting for every block of 10 members, and reports to the board by 7 January each year.
15.4 The Tollbridge depot council has 11 seats, meets on the first Tuesday of each month and is chaired by Sam Kerrigan. A meeting needs 3 members present, and the minutes go to the board within 21 days.
15.4a The Tollbridge council elects 1 delegate(s) to the general meeting for every block of 10 members, and reports to the board by 18 January each year.
15.5 At Wren Hollow the council of 7 seats meets every second Tuesday under the chair, Benedek Holloway. 5 members make a quorum, and 21 days after each meeting the minutes are sent to the board.
15.5a The Wren Hollow council elects 2 delegate(s) to the general meeting for every block of 10 members, and reports to the board by 23 January each year.

18.2 Harbourside receives an operating budget of 200,000 GBP a year. A single item above 2,000 GBP needs the signature of Marit Lindqvist, and above 19,000 GBP a board resolution.

Article 19. Schedule of fees
19.1 The yearly fee for a Small shipper is 400 GBP, reviewed every 2 years.
19.2 The yearly fee for a Regional haulier is 1,250 GBP, reviewed every 3 years.
19.3 The yearly fee for a Network member is 3,100 GBP, reviewed every 4 years.
19.4 The yearly fee for a Associate is 150 GBP, reviewed every 5 years.
