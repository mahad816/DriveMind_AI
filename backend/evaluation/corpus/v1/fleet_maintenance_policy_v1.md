# Fleet maintenance policy, version 1

Version 1 of the policy, form MFC-FM-1, is effective 1 March 2025.

## Oil and filters

Engine oil and filters are changed every 20,000 km or once a year, whichever comes first. The workshop records each change in the fleet register.

## Brakes

Brake pads are inspected every 40,000 km. A vehicle with pads below 3 mm is taken off the road.

## Tyres

Tyres are replaced when the tread falls below 6 mm. Tyre pressures are checked at every pre-trip check.

## Approval

Maintenance spending above 1,500 GBP per vehicle per job needs approval from the workshop foreman.
